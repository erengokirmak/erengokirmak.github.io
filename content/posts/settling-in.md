Hello again,

Today was a fun day at the office.

Registration is done. Using the same ID number from my Bachelor's degree, I've started my Master's. It has been roughly 3 weeks since we set up our PCs in the lab. Three weeks of me not bringing my laptop back home. In the time being, I've befriended the people at the office. They are, as I had suspected, like-minded and friendly people.

I have started to read on my area of research again. I'm still reading Digital Signatures[^1], specifically the part regarding constructions based on the RSA assumption (and its strong variant). It feels weird to learn about a "hard-problem" that we are moving away from as a community. Still, there are a lot of interesting tricks in the signature schemes constructed from this assumption. It is also good to learn about security proofs from the more well-known hard problems before moving onto uncharted territory (for me, at least). However, after finishing the book's discussion of the Random Oracle Model in half an hour, it is a bit disheartening to be stuck trying to understand the mathematics of the RSA-based schemes. No worries though, it isn't anything I can't handle.

Other than that, it is a regular semester at Bilkent. I'm taking four courses which means I'm dealing with classes more than reading for this semester, per the advice of my supervisor. I have high hopes for the months to come :)

[^1]: Katz, Jonathan. Digital Signatures. Springer, 2010.
